About Le Nam Hai Pham

Le Nam Hai Pham received the bachelor’s degree in electrical engineering from the Univer sity of Technology, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam (Dai Hoc Bach Khoa HCM), and the M.Sc. and Ph.D degree in electrical power engineering from the University of South-Eastern Norway, in 2022 and 2025, respectively.

He was an Electrical Engineer with Space Engineering, a power consultant company in Vietnam, from 2017 to 2020. Currently, he is Project Manager of Hitachi Energy Norway, and control Oil & Gas project, transformer station projects in Norway. His research interests include dynamic power systems, cyber-physical energy and power systems, real-time control, and protection in smart grids
